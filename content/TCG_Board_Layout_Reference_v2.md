# The Crazy Game — Board Layout Reference (v2)

**Status: Updated for the Crazy River redesign.** The Mud Pond Loop, Crazy Beach Loop, Bridge, Sharky Shores, and all Smiley/Frowny/Portal/Escape Box positions outside the Crazy River area are unchanged and remain cross-validated against the Master Rules Reference. Segments 5, 6a, and 6b below have been rebuilt to reflect the new Crazy River Junction, the one-way Crazy River path, and its three Crazy Coin Spaces — see the Master Rules Reference, Section 13, and changelog item #52, for the full mechanical rundown.

**Legend:** B = Blank · S = Star · LP = Life Preserver · M = Mud · (−1) = Lose LP space · CC = Crazy Coin space · Smiley/Frowny numbers reflect board order of their *origin* (send) space, independently numbered per type.

## Full Path, Start to Castle

### Segment 1 — Start to Mud Pond Loop junction
Start → B, B, B, B, S, **Smiley 1 (send)**, B, LP, B, B, M → **[Junction: Star]**

### Segment 2 — Mud Pond Loop (Section 4)
**Right branch:** B, B, M, **Frowny 1 (entry)**, B, S, B, M, B, LP *(convergence)*
**Left branch:** B, B, B, B, B **[Frowny 5 entry]**, M, S, B, B, B, LP *(convergence — same space as right branch)*

### Segment 3 — Mud Pond convergence to Crazy Beach Loop junction
**Smiley 2 (send)**, B, *(pass under the Bridge here — not a counted space, see Section 6)*, S, M, B **[Frowny 3 entry]**, B, **[2 OR 3 escape box]**, **Smiley 3 (send)**, B, S → **[Junction: Blank]**

### Segment 4 — Crazy Beach Loop (Section 5) — matches existing documentation exactly
**Right/Lower branch:** B, S, B, S *(+ Lower Crazy Beach entry point)*, B, S, B, LP, **Checkered Portal #1** *(only triggers if landed on exactly — see note below)*
**Left/Upper branch:** B, B, S, B, S *(+ Upper Crazy Beach entry point)*, LP, B, **Smiley 4 (send)**, B, B, **Checkered Portal #1** *(same space, same caveat)*

**Important: this Portal is not a mandatory checkpoint.** Both branches' independent numbering ends at this same space, but like any other space, the Portal only triggers if a roll lands a player exactly on it (Section 11). Most rolls will overshoot it — those players simply continue straight into Segment 5 below, on the single already-merged path, without ever teleporting. Only a player who lands exactly on this space gets sent immediately to Checkered Portal #2 (Segment 7).

### Segment 5 — Continuing past Checkered Portal #1's space (for players who don't land exactly on it) to the Crazy River Junction
M, B, B, S, B *(shared space: **Smiley 4 entry** + **Frowny 2 entry**)*, LP, B, **[Swap space]**, B → **[Crazy River Junction]**

**The Crazy River Junction is not a fork.** Every player passes through or lands on it, and routing is fully automatic based on Life Preserver count — no player choice, no roll requirement. It's also a standard Life Preserver space: landing exactly on it grants a Life Preserver first, before routing is applied. See Master Rules Reference, Section 13, for the full mechanic.

### Segment 6a — Crazy River (Section 13) — one-way, automatic entry with ≥1 Life Preserver
B, B, LP, S, B, (−1), B, **CC**, **CC**, **CC**, B, B, LP, B, S, B, (−1), B, B → *(steps out of Crazy River back onto the main track, at the same convergence point Segment 6b ends at — that space is itself a Life Preserver space)*

19 spaces total. All spaces except the three CC spaces are water for Life Preserver purposes — this includes both Stars and both Life Preserver spaces within Crazy River, not just the (−1) spaces. The three CC spaces are dry.

*Note: the Crazy Coin Stack (the physical card deck players draw from) sits physically between Crazy River and the main path — this is the shared draw pile, separate from the three CC board spaces themselves.*

### Segment 6b — Main path (automatic route for players with 0 Life Preservers at the Junction)
B, M, B, **Fish Pond**, **Spiral Portal #1**, B, S, M, B *(**Smiley 1 entry**)*, B, LP *(convergence — same space as Segment 6a's end)*

### Segment 7 — Convergence to start of Sharky Shores
**Frowny 1 (send)**, S, **[5 OR 6 escape box]**, B, **Frowny 2 (send)**, **Checkered Portal #2**, S, B *(**Smiley 2 entry**)*, LP, B *(**Smiley 3 entry**)*, **Bridge (actual space here — see Section 6)**, B, **Spiral Portal #2**, B *(**Frowny 4 entry**)*, S, **Frowny 3 (send)**, M, **[1 OR 4 escape box]**, LP, B

### Segment 8 — Sharky Shores to Castle (Section 7)
*(Stepping-stone spaces function as normal Blank spaces, just visually distinct)*
B, B, **Frowny 4 (send)**, (−1), **Frowny 5 (send)**, (−1), B, Whirlpool, Whirlpool, Whirlpool → **CASTLE**

## Design Note (Resolved, updated for the Crazy River redesign)

The (−1) spaces are mechanically identical everywhere — no rules difference between Sharky Shores and Crazy River. The original visual distinction (an icon placed boldly in the middle of the space versus off to the side) was based on Risky Rapids' old guaranteed-Life-Preserver-on-entry design, which no longer describes Crazy River the same way: entry is still gated by Life Preserver count, but a player can end up mid-river with zero after a bump, same as always. Whether the physical art for Crazy River's two (−1) spaces should follow the old "bold, centered icon" treatment or something else is a print-asset decision, not a rules question — flagging it here since the underlying assumption that motivated the original choice has changed, even though the rule itself hasn't.
